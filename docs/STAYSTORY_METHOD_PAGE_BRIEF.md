## Page Purpose

Explain **why StayStory works**, what makes the approach different, and how a host moves
from scattered ideas to an intentionally designed guest experience.

This is *not* primarily a product-feature tour — the Platform page goes deeper into
individual tools. This page focuses on: the problem, the shift in thinking, why guest
experience matters, the methodology, how each stage builds on the last, the role of the
Experience Compass, the Guest Journey Playbook as the outcome, and evidence guests feel
the difference.

It answers *"Why should I approach hospitality this way?"* and *"How does StayStory help
me actually do it?"*

## Overall Story

The Problem → The Shift → Why Experiences Matter → The StayStory Method → The Guiding
Thread → The Outcome → Guest Proof → Take Action.

The page becomes progressively more tangible moving down. At the top we are changing how
the visitor thinks; by the bottom they should understand exactly what the system helps
them create.

## Section 1 — Hero

- Label: `THE STAYSTORY METHOD`
- Headline: **Great hospitality isn't accidental. It's designed.**
  - This is StayStory brand language. **Do not** place it in quotation marks or attribute
    it to anyone unless a verified source is supplied later.
- Supporting copy: memorable experiences aren't created by adding more amenities; they're
  intentionally shaped around what guests feel, notice, remember, and talk about after.
- Primary CTA: **Explore the Method** · Secondary CTA: **See the Platform**
- Include the guest-journey visual from the mockup, across stages such as Dreaming,
  Booking, Arriving, Staying, Reflecting, carrying the idea *"Intentional moments that
  create impact"*.
- The visual leads toward a Guest Journey Playbook preview, which may include the title
  **Guest Journey Playbook**, the line *"A complete guide to the experience you design."*
  and a CTA **Open Your Playbook**.

## Section 2 — The Problem

- Label: `THE PROBLEM` · Headline: **Most hosts ask the wrong question.**
- A clear visual contrast, both sides editable:
  - *Traditional question* — **What else should I add?** (more amenities, bigger TV,
    better coffee machine, more towels, higher thread count)
  - *StayStory question* — **What do I want guests to remember?** (feeling welcomed,
    cared for, at home, connected, inspired)
- Do not over-explain. The purpose is to make the difference in thinking obvious.

## Section 3 — The Shift

- Label: `THE SHIFT` · Headline: **From providing more to designing what matters.**
- Simple visual transformations: Amenities → Moments · Property → Journey ·
  Service → Feeling
- Bridges problem and method. Make clear StayStory does not tell hosts to stop caring
  about service or amenities — it places them inside a larger intentional experience.
- Highly visual and concise.

## Section 4 — Why Experiences Matter

- Label: `WHY EXPERIENCES MATTER`
- Core idea: *Guests don't remember everything. They remember what stood out — and how it
  made them feel.*
- Supporting concepts: anticipation, meaningful details, rituals, surprise, story,
  emotional peaks.
- Philosophy without an essay. No decorative photography. Prefer typography, small visual
  tags, icons, experience diagrams, whitespace.

## Section 5 — The Method

- Label: `THE METHOD` · Headline: **A repeatable way to design the guest journey.**
- One connected sequence, not unrelated feature cards:
  1. **Share** — provide property details and complete the Experience Audit. Surfaces the
     property, the existing journey, what works, friction, missed opportunities, and what
     makes the stay distinctive. *Do not* describe this as "connecting a listing".
  2. **Define** — build the Experience Compass: what the experience should feel like, and
     the principles guiding future decisions.
  3. **Design** — shape the journey with the Experience Blueprint, Generator and Story
     Builder. Intentions become moments, rituals, touches, guest-facing language, journey
     decisions.
  4. **Deliver** — bring it together in the Guest Journey Playbook, a usable guide for
     delivering the experience consistently.
- Must read as **Share → Define → Design → Deliver**, each building on the last. Use
  directional flow, connecting lines, or another understated system treatment.

## Section 6 — The Guiding Thread

- Label: `THE GUIDING THREAD`
- Headline: **Every decision comes back to what you want guests to feel.**
- Clarifies the Experience Compass as the guiding layer keeping the experience coherent —
  not another isolated feature.
- Show the Compass connected to illustrative, editable principles: Effortless · Warm ·
  Local & Immersive · Thoughtful.
- The Compass answers *"Does this decision support the experience we're trying to create?"*
- Clean diagram or central Compass visual. Not overly technical.

## Section 7 — The Outcome

- Label: `THE OUTCOME` · Headline: **One living Guest Journey Playbook.**
- Everything from the Method comes together in one usable, shareable guide.
- Substantial product preview, which may include Overview, Journey, Moments, Details,
  Team Notes, Resources, and may reference Dreaming / Booking / Arriving / Staying /
  Reflecting. Structure may evolve — every label editable.
- Supporting copy: *A complete, shareable guide that keeps your team aligned and your
  experience consistent across every stay.*
- Preview sits in a replaceable interface/image container.

## Section 8 — Guests Feel It / Proof

- Label: `GUESTS FEEL IT` · Headline: **You can hear the difference in what guests say.**
- A small set of guest-review excerpts — concise, not a duplicate of the homepage Proof
  section. Focus on feeling welcomed, thoughtful details, care, ease, personal touches,
  emotional memory.
- **Do not invent testimonials.** Use placeholders until verified guest language exists.

## Section 9 — Final CTA

- Headline: **Ready to design the experience intentionally?**
- Primary: **Start Free** · Secondary: **Explore the Platform**
- Reassurance: *No credit card · Setup in minutes · Cancel anytime*
- Reuse the homepage's closing CTA treatment so the site stays cohesive. Introduce no new
  concept here.

## Visual Density

Several ideas live on this page, so density control matters. Keep every section, but do
not compress them. Use more vertical spacing, clear separation, generous margins,
intentional whitespace, varied section heights, strong hierarchy. Improve pacing rather
than deleting meaningful sections to shorten the page.

## Photography Rule

Photography should be minimal, and **none** in the Problem, Shift, Why Experiences Matter,
Method, or Guiding Thread sections. Those rely on typography, icons, diagrams, cards,
product previews, comparison layouts and whitespace. Any photography must have a clear
explanatory purpose. The Playbook product preview matters more than decorative property
photography.

## Editable Page Structure

Section labels, headlines, supporting copy, comparison lists, method step names and
descriptions, icons, product previews, Compass principles, Playbook labels, guest quotes,
CTAs and links must all remain editable. Components modular and reusable where practical.
Do not flatten significant content into static artwork.

## Responsive Behaviour

Desktop, tablet and mobile. On mobile preserve the sequence: Hero → Problem → Shift → Why
Experiences Matter → Share → Define → Design → Deliver → Guiding Thread → Guest Journey
Playbook → Guest Proof → Final CTA. Use stacking and spacing rather than shrinking.

## Final Page Goal

The visitor should understand that StayStory is not about adding more things to a
property. It is a repeatable method for intentionally designing what guests experience,
feel and remember — and that the practical result is a living system they can use to
deliver that experience consistently.

## Working Method

Build section by section, beginning with the Hero. Do not build the whole page at once.
